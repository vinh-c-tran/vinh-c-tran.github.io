Research experience

Aug 2023 – Present

Graduate Student Researcher

Sanchez-Yamagishi Lab · University of California, Irvine

Lithography & exposure optimization

  • Designed multilayer CAD layouts and patterned resist masks by electron-beam lithography (EBL) and direct-write photolithography for injection molds, device etching, and contact definition.
  • Produced features down to 100 nm by SEM-based EBL, tuning focus, beam alignment and current, layer-specific dose, and write magnification.
  • Executed blind EBL writes across a wafer using three-point leveling and pattern fracturing.

Etching & contact fabrication

  • Patterned SiO₂/Si injection molds using wet etching and reactive-ion etching (RIE), including Hall-bar, Corbino, and approximately 150 nm nanowire geometries.
  • Fabricated seven bismuth transport devices using EBL, SF₆ RIE, ion-mill contact cleaning, in situ metal deposition, and lift-off.

Thin-film growth & heterostructures

  • Developed growth of 10–100 nm single-crystal metal films directly inside hexagonal boron nitride (hBN), controlling thickness and geometry while protecting against oxidation. Grew more than 50 bismuth, tin, indium, and lead samples.
  • Extended van der Waals injection molding to integrate superconducting lead films with graphene contacts.
Van der Waals Injection Molding project

Structural & electrical characterization

  • Assessed thickness, uniformity, and roughness by AFM, and crystallinity by TEM imaging and diffraction. Evaluated residual resistivity, residual resistance ratio, and mobility through cryogenic magnetotransport.
  • Observed 1.6–1.8× zero-bias conductance enhancement in superconductor–graphene junctions using cryogenic lock-in measurements, DC bias, and electrostatic gating.

Measurement systems & documentation

  • Automated acquisition with Python/QCoDeS and SQL data logging; built Quarto reports capturing plots, experimental settings, and metadata.
  • Led installation and initial testing of a 9 T, 1.5 K Cryomagnetics pulse-tube cryostat, and maintain its vacuum, compressor, probe, and gas-handling systems.
